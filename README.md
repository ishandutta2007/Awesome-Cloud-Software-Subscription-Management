## 🔓 Open-Source GitHub Projects



*Sorted by GitHub_Stars_Count (Descending)* 🌟



- **[Wallos](https://github.com/ellite/Wallos)** [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/ellite/Wallos?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/ellite/Wallos/stargazers)  

  **Open-source personal subscription tracker**, GPL-3.0 licensed. **The most popular open-source subscription management tool** with **5K+ GitHub stars** . **Track all subscriptions in one place** — recurring expenses, payment cycles, and renewal dates . **40+ currencies** with automatic exchange rate updates. **Multi-language support** (20+ languages). **Category organization** and **custom payment methods** . **Email notifications** for upcoming renewals. **Statistical dashboards** with spending trends. **Docker deployment** — `docker run -d -p 80:80 -v ./data:/var/www/html/data -v ./logos:/var/www/html/images/uploads/logos --restart unless-stopped bellamy/wallos:latest` . **The most complete open-source subscription tracker** for personal and small business use. 📊



- **[SubTrackr](https://github.com/Ashfaaq98/SubTrackr)** [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/Ashfaaq98/SubTrackr?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/Ashfaaq98/SubTrackr/stargazers)  

  **Subscription and expense tracking API**, open-source. **Go-based REST API** for managing recurring subscriptions and expenses . **User authentication** with JWT. **CRUD operations** for subscriptions, expenses, and users . **Docker deployment** with `docker-compose up` . **The most developer-friendly open-source subscription API**. 🔧



- **[Subscriptions Manager](https://github.com/bmuschko/subscriptions-manager)** [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/bmuschko/subscriptions-manager?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/bmuschko/subscriptions-manager/stargazers)  

  **Application to keep track of subscriptions**, open-source. **Go-based CLI** for managing subscriptions . **Simple and focused** — add, list, and track subscription costs . **The simplest open-source subscription manager CLI**. 🖥️



- **[subtracker](https://github.com/markdouthwaite/subtracker)** [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/markdouthwaite/subtracker?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/markdouthwaite/subtracker/stargazers)  

  **Minimal subscription tracker**, open-source. **Lightweight and focused** — track recurring subscriptions with **simple commands** . **The most minimal open-source subscription tracker**. 📋



- **[InvoicePlane](https://github.com/InvoicePlane/InvoicePlane)** [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/InvoicePlane/InvoicePlane?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/InvoicePlane/InvoicePlane/stargazers)  

  **Open-source invoicing and billing management**, MIT licensed. **Recurring invoices** and **subscription management** . **Client management** and **payment tracking** . **The most mature open-source invoicing with subscription support** . 📄



- **[Kill Bill](https://github.com/killbill/killbill)** [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/killbill/killbill?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/killbill/killbill/stargazers)  

  **Open-source subscription billing and payment platform**, Apache-2.0 licensed. **Subscription management**, **invoicing**, **payment processing**, and **analytics** in a unified platform . **Plugin architecture** for extensibility. **Java-based with REST APIs** . **The most mature open-source subscription billing engine** for SaaS and digital services . 💰



- **[Lago](https://github.com/getlago/lago)** [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/getlago/lago?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/getlago/lago/stargazers)  

  **Open-source metering and usage-based billing**, AGPL-3.0 licensed. **Metering, billing, and analytics** for usage-based and subscription models . **Flexible pricing** — subscription, usage-based, and hybrid . **REST API and webhooks** for integration . **The most modern open-source billing platform** for SaaS . 🦭



- **[Chargebee Alternatives (Open-Source)](https://github.com/getlago/lago)** [![Stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/getlago/lago?style=social&color=white)](https://github.com/getlago/lago/stargazers)  

  **Open-source alternatives to Chargebee, Recurly, and Stripe Billing** — **Lago** for usage-based billing, **Kill Bill** for subscription billing, and **InvoicePlane** for invoicing . **The complete open-source billing stack** for SaaS companies . 🔄

## ⚠️ Disclaimer



- This is a **community-curated** list — not exhaustive and not an endorsement. ℹ️

- **SaaS management platforms vary significantly in pricing** — **Zylo** at **$50K–$300K/year** , **Productiv** from **$25K/year** , **Torii** from **$8K/year** , and **Cledara** from **$99/month** . **Choose based on company size and whether you need discovery, optimization, or procurement negotiation** .

- **AWS Marketplace Subscriptions is free** — it provides consolidated billing and subscription visibility for marketplace purchases . **Private Marketplaces** add organizational procurement controls .

- **Open-source tools (Wallos, SubTrackr, Kill Bill) are not turnkey** — **Wallos requires Docker deployment** with data and logo volume mounts . **Kill Bill requires Java and database administration** . **Always validate subscription tracking and billing workflows with a proof-of-concept** before production deployment . 📋
